# Remote Access Policy

## Section 1: Purpose

This policy defines requirements for employees accessing Contoso systems from outside a
corporate office.

## Section 2: Eligibility

Remote access is available to all full-time employees and to contractors whose statement of
work explicitly grants remote access. Eligibility is configured by HR/People Systems, not
requested through this assistant.

## Section 3: Required Tools

Remote work requires, at minimum:

- The corporate VPN client (Contoso Secure Connect), used whenever accessing internal-only
  systems (file shares, internal ticketing, internal wikis).
- MFA enrollment.
- A device meeting the Corporate Device Policy.

Internet-facing SaaS tools approved for business use (e.g., approved email and chat clients)
may be used without VPN, since they do not require the internal network.

## Section 4: Data Protection While Remote

- Confidential or Restricted data must not be stored on personal cloud storage.
- Screens must be locked when the device is unattended, including in public spaces.
- Printing Confidential/Restricted documents at home is discouraged and must be securely
  destroyed if done.

## Section 5: Loss or Theft

A lost or stolen device used for remote access must be reported immediately per the Security
Guidelines document so it can be remotely locked/wiped.
