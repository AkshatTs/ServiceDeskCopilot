# Password Policy

## Section 1: Purpose

This policy defines password and credential requirements for all Contoso accounts.

## Section 2: Minimum Requirements

- Minimum 14 characters.
- Must not match any of the employee's last 10 passwords.
- Must not contain the employee's username or full name.
- Passphrases are recommended over short complex strings.

## Section 3: Rotation

Passwords must be changed every 180 days. Accounts with administrative privileges must be
changed every 90 days. The identity portal sends a reminder 14 days before expiry.

## Section 4: Prohibited Practices

- Passwords must never be written down in plain text, shared over email or chat, or stored in
  unencrypted files.
- **No employee, IT support analyst, administrator, or automated assistant is ever permitted
  to ask for, view, store, or repeat another user's password.** IT support can trigger a
  password reset link sent to the employee's registered device; support staff and this
  assistant never learn or need to know the password itself.
- Shared/group logins are prohibited except for pre-approved service accounts managed by IT.

## Section 5: Reporting

If an employee believes their password has been compromised, they must change it immediately
and report the incident per the Security Guidelines document.
